# ai-risk-register
The business scenario: an AI risk register for T and A Technology LLC, built with fictitious data.
How risks are scored: likelihood × impact, inherent vs. residual, and the risk appetite of 8.
Which frameworks it maps to: NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and GDPR.
Two or three findings, such as how many risks are still outside appetite and why.
